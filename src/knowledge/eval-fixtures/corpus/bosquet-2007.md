# Effects of tapering on performance: a meta-analysis

## Taper length

Pooling the controlled studies, a taper lasting 8 to 14 days gave the largest performance benefit. Shorter tapers left residual fatigue, and tapers longer than about three weeks started to lose fitness. On average, a well-designed taper improved performance by about 2-3%.

## Volume, intensity and frequency

Training volume should be cut progressively by 41-60% of the pre-taper level. Training intensity should be maintained: dropping the hard efforts removed much of the benefit. Training frequency should stay the same or fall only slightly, since fewer sessions did not help. A progressive, non-linear reduction worked better than a step taper where volume drops all at once.

## Practical application

Keep short high-intensity efforts in the plan during the taper, shorten sessions rather than skipping them, and avoid adding new stimuli in the final two weeks before the main race.
