# Brick workouts for triathletes

## The heavy-legs problem

Running straight after cycling feels awkward: legs feel heavy or like jelly, stride is short and pace feels harder than usual. The feeling comes from the change in muscle recruitment and blood flow between pedalling and running, and it fades with specific practice.

## How to train it

A brick is a bike session followed directly by a run. Most bricks only need a short run of 10-20 minutes off the bike at goal race effort. Focus on quick, light steps with a high cadence for the first kilometre, and let the stride lengthen once the legs settle. Raising pedalling cadence in the last minutes of the ride also helps.

## Placement in the plan

One brick a week during the build phase is enough for most age-groupers. Practise the transition too: lay out gear, rack the bike and change shoes quickly, so race-day transitions are routine.
