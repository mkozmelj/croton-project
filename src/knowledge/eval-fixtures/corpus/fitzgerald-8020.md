# The 80/20 approach for recreational athletes

## The moderate-intensity rut

Recreational runners and triathletes tend to train too hard on easy days. Surveys of amateur runners show them spending about half of their time at moderate intensity, in the grey zone between easy and hard, which leaves them too tired for truly hard sessions.

## Finding the easy ceiling without a lab

The first ventilatory threshold marks the top of low intensity. Without lab testing, the talk test is a good guide: if you can speak in full sentences comfortably, you are below it. Heart rate at about 80% of lactate threshold heart rate, or perceived effort of 4 out of 10 or lower, gives similar limits.

## Counting time, not sessions

80/20 is counted by time: about 80% of training minutes at low intensity and 20% at moderate or high intensity. Slowing down the easy sessions is usually the single most effective change for amateurs who have plateaued.
