# Notes on Daniels' running paces

## Training paces from VDOT

VDOT from a recent race sets five training paces. Easy pace is conversational and makes up most of the weekly mileage. Marathon pace is used for steady long efforts. Threshold pace is comfortably hard, about the pace a runner could hold for roughly an hour in a race. Interval pace is close to the pace for about 10-12 minutes of racing, targeting VO2max. Repetition pace is faster still and targets speed and running economy.

## Workout limits

Threshold running per session should total no more than about 10% of weekly mileage, either as a 20-minute tempo run or as cruise intervals with one minute of rest. Interval work should stay under 8% of weekly mileage or 10 km, whichever is less, with work bouts of three to five minutes and recoveries equal to or shorter than the work. Repetition work should stay under 5% of weekly mileage, with full recovery between short fast reps.

## Adjusting paces

Paces are raised only after several weeks at a given VDOT feel easy, or after a new race result. Heat, altitude and hills call for effort-based running rather than chasing pace.
