# Critical swim speed training

## Finding CSS

Critical swim speed approximates the pace a swimmer can sustain for about 30 minutes. It comes from a 400 m and a 200 m time trial: the difference between the two times divided by two gives the pace per 100 m.

## Main sets at CSS pace

Classic CSS sets are repeats of 100 m to 400 m at CSS pace with short rests, for example 10 x 100 m with 10-15 seconds rest, or 5 x 200 m with 20 seconds rest. A tempo trainer or pace clock helps keep every repeat even. The session should feel hard but controlled, and the last repeat should be as fast as the first.

## Technique first

For adult-onset swimmers, stroke technique limits speed more than fitness does. Drills, a relaxed breathing rhythm and good body position should be part of most sessions, and CSS sets are added once the stroke holds together at pace. Retest CSS every six to eight weeks.
