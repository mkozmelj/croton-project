# Preparing for downhill running in mountain races

## Why descents hurt

Running downhill loads the quadriceps eccentrically: the muscle lengthens while braking each step. Unaccustomed eccentric load causes muscle damage and delayed-onset soreness, and in long mountain races destroyed quads often end the race before fitness does.

## The repeated bout effect

A first exposure to hard downhill running protects the muscle against damage from the next bouts for weeks. Athletes should include progressive downhill running from about six to eight weeks before a race with large descents, starting with short controlled descents and building to long steep ones. The last big descent session should be about ten days before the race.

## Strength and technique

Eccentric strength work such as step-downs, split squats and slow lunges supports the specific training. On technical terrain, short quick steps, relaxed arms and eyes a few metres ahead reduce braking forces. Poles take some load off the legs on steep descents if the athlete has practised with them.
