# Strength training for endurance athletes

## Benefits

Heavy strength training improves running and cycling economy and the ability to produce force late in long events. Improvements of a few percent in economy have been shown after 8-12 weeks of two sessions per week.

## Heavy loads, few reps

The effective programs use heavy loads for few repetitions, for example three to four sets of four to six reps of squats, deadlifts or step-ups, plus explosive work such as jumps. Because volume is low and endurance training continues, endurance athletes do not gain meaningful muscle bulk or body mass.

## Scheduling

Put strength sessions on the same day as hard endurance sessions, a few hours after, so that easy days stay easy. Keep two sessions a week in the base phase, one maintenance session in the build and race phases, and drop heavy lifting in the final taper week.
