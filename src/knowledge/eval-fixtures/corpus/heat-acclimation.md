# Heat acclimation before a hot race

## Adaptations

Repeated exercise in the heat expands plasma volume, increases sweat rate, lowers the sodium content of sweat and lowers heart rate and core temperature at a given workload. Most of the adaptation happens within 10-14 days.

## Protocols

Training for 60-90 minutes a day in hot conditions for about two weeks acclimatises most athletes. Where it is not hot outside, overdressing during indoor sessions or taking a hot bath of around 40 °C for 30-40 minutes right after training are practical alternatives.

## Keeping the adaptation

Acclimation fades over a few weeks without heat exposure, so it should be timed to finish shortly before travel to the race, with a few top-up sessions if the gap is longer. Reduce training intensity in the first days of heat exposure.
